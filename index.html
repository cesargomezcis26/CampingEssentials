02 / A LITTLE TRAIL WISDOM

Good habits. Great trips.

You don’t need to know everything.
These are a good place to start.

01 — BEFORE YOU GO

A little homework pays off.

Check weather, campsite reservations, fire restrictions, and local wildlife advice. Tell someone where you’re going and when you’ll be back.

02 — AT CAMP

Settle in before sunset.

Choose a designated, level campsite. Set up your tent while it’s light, keep food in approved storage, and keep cooking away from sleeping areas.

03 — WHEN YOU LEAVE

Leave only the good memories.

Pack out every bit of trash. Respect wildlife and other campers. If fires are permitted, fully extinguish yours with water until the ashes are cold.
